Privacy Policy

Pagecraft · Last updated 13 November 2026

Pagecraft is designed so that your documents stay on your device. We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.

What we collect

Nothing. Pagecraft has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The app does not send your documents, photos or any other information to us or to third parties.

On-device processing

Scanning, text recognition (OCR), compression, AI upscaling, redaction, form filling and automatic naming all run locally on your iPhone or iPad using Apple frameworks. Your files are stored in the app's own folder, which you can see in the Files app.

Permissions

  • Camera: only to scan documents when you choose to.
  • Photos: only the photos you pick are read; saving edited images to Photos is optional.
  • Face ID: only if you turn on App Lock. Biometric data is handled by iOS and never accessible to the app.

"My Info"

Details you enter for form filling are saved only on your device and can be changed or deleted at any time.

iCloud and sharing

If you share a document or save it to iCloud Drive or another app, that transfer is handled by iOS and the service you choose.

Children

Pagecraft does not collect data from anyone, including children.
