Quick answers

Is it really free? Yes: no subscription, no watermarks, no limits.

Where are my files? In the Files app under On My iPhone › Pagecraft.

How do I reorder pages? Hold a page or photo until it lifts, then drag. Or tap the Arrange button.

How do I add pages to a PDF? Open the PDF and tap +. New pages go into that PDF.

How do I make a PDF smaller? Long-press it in the library › Compress. Pick a mode or set "Under X MB".

Are my documents uploaded anywhere? No. Everything runs on your device. See the privacy policy.
